
Create a process flow for assigning IT help desk tickets.
- Steps:
  - "Ticket submitted by user"
  - "Categorize issue" (priority: low, medium, high)
  - "Assign to appropriate technician"
  - "Technician resolves issue"
  - "Ticket closed and feedback collected"
- Decision Points:
  - "Is the issue resolved?" > Yes: "Close ticket" > No: "Reassign to a different technician"
