
Create a flowchart for shopping cart checkout in a food delivery app.
- Start: "User adds items to cart"
- Steps:
  - "View cart and confirm items"
  - "Proceed to checkout"
  - "Enter delivery address"
  - "Choose payment method"
  - "Review order"
- End: "Order placed"
- Decision Points:
  - "Is payment valid?" > Yes: "Process order" > No: "Prompt user to re-enter payment"
